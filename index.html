01 / THE IDEA

A registry, not a platform tax.

Greenways is designed to sit underneath existing tools. It observes the lifecycle of work, records meaningful events and gives participants a durable account of what happened.

02 / FIRST USES

Start where attribution
already breaks.

01

Campaign records

Track content, briefs, versions and contributors across a marketing campaign. Give managers a complete audit trail without asking every participant to maintain one.

  • Automatic contribution capture
  • Asset and version lineage
  • Campaign-level audit export
02

Music provenance

Preserve the history around a track—from sessions and stems to splits, releases and licences—so credit remains attached to the work.

  • Session and recording history
  • Contributor attribution
  • Rights and release records
03

Trusted spaces

Create digital spaces with their own scoped rules, credentials and public records. Greenways governs the space itself, not the rest of the internet.

  • Accreditation and roles
  • Transparent local rules
  • Portable reputation records

03 / PRINCIPLES

Invisible by default

The record should emerge from the workflow—not become another task inside it.

Scoped authority

A Greenways space can make rules for itself, while remaining legible to the outside world.

Durable attribution

Credit, provenance and decisions stay attached to the work as tools and teams change.

Open foundations

Core infrastructure is built in public, including Hara, Greenways’ language and runtime project.

04 / BUILDING IN PUBLIC

Greenways is taking shape.

The first pilots focus on campaign tracking and music attribution. The underlying systems are being developed openly through the Greenways GitHub organisation.
